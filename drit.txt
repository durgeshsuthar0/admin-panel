🚀 A Good Pre-Launch → Launch Content Flow
If you're starting from zero, I wouldn't immediately fill your Instagram with product photos.

I'd build the account like this:

Phase 1 — Mystery
“Something is coming.”

Post:

Logo teaser
Fabric close-up
Printing close-up
Blurred product
Brand slogan
Stories:

Polls
Guessing games
Countdown
BTS
Phase 2 — Reveal
“We are a custom clothing brand.”

Post:

Brand reveal
What you do
First product reveal
Customization concept
How it works
Phase 3 — Diwali Build-Up 🪔
“This Diwali, wear your own design.”

Post:

Diwali collection
Product photos
Custom designs
Launch date
Launch offer
Reels showing the printing process
Stories:

Countdown
Design voting
Q&A
Sneak peeks
“Launching in 5 days…”
Phase 4 — Launch 🚀
Your launch-day content could be:

Morning:
“IT'S FINALLY HERE 🎉”

Afternoon:
Reel showing your store/product/printing process.

Evening:
“Your design. Your T-shirt. Your style.”

Night:
Customer/order/offer reminder.

💡 Some Strong Content Series Names
Instead of random posts, create recurring series so your page has a recognizable structure:

#MadeByYou — customer/custom designs
#PrintOfTheDay — one design every day
#BehindThePrint — printing process
#DesignItYourWay — customization ideas
#FreshOffThePress — newly printed orders
#WearYourIdea — brand storytelling
#FromIdeaToTee — customer design → finished product
#PrintCheck — quality/print close-ups
#TeeTalk — tips, trends and clothing content
One important positioning idea
Don't market yourself simply as “a T-shirt printing shop.”

Try to build the brand around:

“You imagine it. We print it.”

or

“Your idea. Your design. Your T-shirt.”

That gives you a much bigger content universe than simply posting pictures of printed T-shirts.
